First Set
=========
1. Play on
----------
The Kesh Jig (G)x2
4 bars bodhran; 4 bars drone (wind); Everybody in x 2 
1 min 20 secs

2. Virginia Reel (Go-round)
---------------------------
Oh Susanna (G)x2 / Brighton Camp (G)x2 / The Rose Tree (D)x2 -> b
4 bars in; Final note repeat and hold 
3 mins 30 secs

3. The Quaker's Wife
--------------------
The Quaker's Wife (D)x2 / The Old Favourite (G)x2 / Garret Barry's (D)x2/ Lisnagun (G)x2 -> d
4 bars in; Final note, repeat, and hold
4 mins 40 secs

4. Oxo Reel (new)
-----------------
Quebec Donkey (G)x2 / Dennis Murphy’s (D)x2 / John Ryan's (D)x2 -> D
4 bars in; John Ryan's 1st time A part bodhran solo; 2nd time all; Outro: stabsx2
4 mins 40 secs

5.Sweets of May (Go-round)
--------------------------
Mooncoin Jig (A) x2 / Seanamhac Tube Station (Gdor)x2 -> c#
4 bars in

6. I Want To Be Near You
------------------------
4 bars in
D x 4; G x 2 (stop); A x 1; done twice
2 mins 20 secs









Second Set
==========

1. Play on Ciel d’automne (D)x2 / 3rd time A part + A part bars 5-8
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Intro: Piano, melody instruments tremolo E-F# or B-C#
1st time: A1 trumpet + tuba | A2 all brass | B1 all | B2 all
2nd time: A1 all | A2 all | B1 all | B2 all melody + tuba 
3rd time: A1 melody + tuba + trombone | A2 bars 5-8 only, slowing down

2. All The Way To Galway (Go-round)
-----------------------------------
All The Way To Galway (D)x2 / Tralee Gaol (Am) 2nd time claps / Egan's (D)x2 -> A
4 bars in; Final note repeat and hold.
3 mins 30 secs

3. Bridge of Athlone (Go-round)
-------------------------------
Coppers and Brass (G)x2 / Dusty Windowsills (Am)x2 / Banish Misfortune (G)x2 -> b
4 bars in
5 mins 30 secs

4. Blaydon Races  (Go-round)
----------------------------
Blaydon Races (C)x2 / Keep your feet still Geordie Hinney (G)x2 / Blaydon Races (G)x2 / (A)xn
Start speed up in Blaydon (G)

5. Cumberland Square
--------------------
The Centenary (D)x2 (2 A parts, 2 B parts) / Lark In The Morning (D)x2
4 bars in
4 mins 20 secs

6. Circassian  (Go-round on Pachelbel) only
-------------------------------------------

--------------
